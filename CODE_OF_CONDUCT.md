# Code of Conduct

We aim for a welcoming, respectful project space. Be considerate, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and code. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation when needed. For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately through the hosting forge's reporting mechanism once available; do not include private household data in a public report.
